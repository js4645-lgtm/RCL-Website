Case studies

Case studies are the starting point for building a broader evidence-base. Before modelling avoided loss in the abstract, each case reconstructs a specific event: what was invested beforehand, what happened, and what it cost or saved on the books afterward. Sources are triangulated across multiple sources so that no single disclosure or report is the sole source of the case.

Financial case studies

A new set of cases is under development by the Lab, built from corporate disclosures, company filings, and ratings agency analysis. Each one puts a number on what mitigation did, or failed to do, to a balance sheet after a real event.
