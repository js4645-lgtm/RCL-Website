The Investor's Primer on Nature Standards: The Integrity Layer

Investors do not lack nature metrics. They have more than they can use, and no agreed way to tell which ones would survive diligence. The primer starts from the question capital allocators actually face: how do you navigate that landscape to structure deals that deliver verified nature impact and still deliver returns? It answers with a three-lens filter for the pre-deal phase. Data: observed State of Nature outcomes over activity counts like trees planted. Deal: an External Rate of Return (ERR), read alongside the IRR, that writes ecological performance into pricing and covenants. Ecosystem: alignment with national biodiversity plans and the Global Biodiversity Framework. What holds investors back is discernment, not data, and the frameworks to act on that already exist.

Miren Gil-Vernet Pagonabarraga and Johan Lopez · PDF
